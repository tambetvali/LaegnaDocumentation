# Why AI interpretations constantly reverse spiritual and material dimensions in Laegna notation

The reversal is not, in itself, an AI mistake.

Laegna is deliberately built so that the same structural relationship can be projected from different sides: inside or outside, local or global, short-term or long-term, lower-rank or higher-rank, material or spiritual. The notation therefore does not contain a single universal visual orientation in which "material must always be here" and "spiritual must always be there."

The AI nevertheless tends to choose one orientation very consistently. It has a strong scientific and linguistic prior in which matter is treated as the externally observable reality, while mind, meaning, intention and spirituality are treated as internal representations. When it encounters Laegna's reversible projections, it therefore frequently selects the material-scientific orientation as its default.

That is why the AI can appear to "reverse" your notation while actually producing a legitimate Laegna projection.

> Expression: Laegna permits the same relation to be read from opposite sides.

> Truth: A reversed projection is not automatically a contradiction. The question is whether the axes remain internally consistent after the projection.

"symbol → structural relation → projection → contextual meaning."

That difference alone explains much of the apparent reversal.
