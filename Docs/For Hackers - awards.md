### How to Solve It: George Pólya's 4-Step Problem-Solving Framework

George Pólya’s landmark 1945 book, **"How to Solve It,"** outlines a systematic, elegant process for breaking down complex problems. While originally designed for mathematics, this framework serves as a universal blueprint for coding, engineering, business, and everyday decision-making.

---

#### The Four Core Phases

##### 1. Understand the Problem
Before attempting a solution, you must completely grasp the scope and boundaries of the challenge.
* **The Unknown:** What exactly are you trying to find, create, or solve?
* **The Data:** What information, resources, or values do you currently have?
* **The Condition:** What are the constraints, rules, or criteria that must be satisfied? Is the data sufficient to satisfy them?
* **Action Item:** Try restating the problem in your own words or sketch a visual diagram.

##### 2. Devise a Plan
This phase requires finding the hidden thread connecting your available data to the unknown goal. Pólya recommends using **heuristics** (mental strategies):
* **Prior Experience:** Have you seen this exact problem before? Or a slightly different version of it?
* **Simplify:** Can you solve a simpler, smaller, or specialized version of the problem first?
* **Decompose:** Can you break the problem into manageable sub-steps?
* **Work Backwards:** Start with the desired outcome and trace the steps required to get there from your current state.

##### 3. Carry Out the Plan
This is the execution phase. Turn your strategy into concrete steps.
* **Precision:** Monitor each action or calculation to ensure it is logically sound.
* **Patience:** If a step fails, do not blindly force it forward. Pause, identify the flaw, and adjust your approach.
* **Verification:** Prove to yourself that each milestone achieved in your plan is correct.

##### 4. Look Back (Reflect)
The problem-solving process does not end when you find the answer. True growth happens during reflection.
* **Validation:** Does the final result make logical sense? Can you easily double-check the work?
* **Efficiency:** Is there a cleaner, faster, or more elegant way to reach the same conclusion?
* **Scalability:** Can this specific solution or method be used to solve future, related problems?

---

#### Quick Reference Summary

| Phase | Core Objective | Key Question to Ask Yourself |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Understand** | Define the boundaries. | "What am I missing, and what am I given?" |
| **2. Plan** | Find a strategy. | "Have I solved something similar to this before?" |
| **3. Execute** | Implement the steps. | "Can I prove that this step is absolutely correct?" |
| **4. Reflect** | Review and adapt. | "Can I use this exact method for other problems?" |
